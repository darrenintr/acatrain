  • Stats live in one hairline-divided strip, never in separate tiles.
  • Subject colour appears only on the shape badge and its progress; titles stay neutral.
  • One decorative motion per screen, at most. Shapes stay still unless they mean “live”.
  • Rows: 60–72 tall, 44 badge, one title line, one meta line, one trailing element.
