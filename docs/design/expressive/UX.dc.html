Screen flow

Ergonomics & accessibility

Reach
OK
Easy · actions
  • One primary action per screen, placed in the bottom third where the thumb rests (Start, Show answer, Continue).
  • Targets ≥ 48 dp; icon buttons have a label for screen readers and a tooltip on hover.
  • Text scales to 200 %: rows grow taller, titles wrap to two lines, nothing truncates the answer text.
  • Contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1 for all text; captions use on-surface-variant, never lighter.
  • Reading order follows the visual order; counters and results are announced politely (aria-live).
  • Undo over confirm: “Again” and “Got it” are reversible from Review; no “are you sure?” dialogs.
  • Copy is short and kind: say what happened, then what to do. No blame on wrong answers.
